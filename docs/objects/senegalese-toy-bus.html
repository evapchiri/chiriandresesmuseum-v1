A vibrant little bus made from wood and card, mimicking the colourful ‘car rapides’ seen everywhere in cities like Saint-Louis. It was given to my mother around 2019 by a dear friend and former colleague — a Spaniard from Cádiz living as an expat in Senegal, who helped her both personally and professionally.

There's a good chance it belonged to his young son first, which is why it has that lovely, personal ‘played-with’ feel. My mother treasures it not only as a reminder of that friendship, but of her first steps in her career, and the support she received at the time.

A souvenir purchased by my parents on their first ever trip together as a couple, in the Medina of Rabat. This traditional steatite carving, normally associated with the Berber culture of the Anti-Atlas, was historically favoured because soapstone is exceptionally heat-resistant — an ideal medium for lighting objects like this one.

The shopkeeper insisted it was a ‘ceremonial Berber artefact’, but my mother was sure that was said to every tourist passing by. Sacred object or modern souvenir, it remains a memento from the very beginning of my parents' relationship.

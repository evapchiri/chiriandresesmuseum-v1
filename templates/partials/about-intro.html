What is Chiriandreses Museum?

"An object in a museum is a condensed story,

a silent witness waiting for a listener."

Sandra Dudley
Museum Materialities: Objects, Sense and Feeling (2009)

Chiriandreses Museum is the culmination of a personal, end-to-end 3D digitisation project of my family's heirlooms and travel mementos — a portal where you can explore and interact with these objects, learn about my family's stories encapsulated in them, as well as their digitisation process.

Every object was captured, processed, and documented as a museum object - following the guidance offered by the 3D4CH centre and the European Commission's VIGIE 2020/654 framework to achieve high-quality models and traceability in 3D digitisation project, adapted in this case for a team of one. The goal was to achieve a high-standard, end-to-end documented process, with an honest account of what was measured, what was estimated, and what objects didn't survive the process, and why.

You can learn here about the project from two angles - the pure technical record, and my personal learning journey behind it:

  • The technical details contains an overview of the hardware, software, and other techincal information about the project. However it is in the Project archive where you will find all the outputted paradata and metadata documents from the project and each object digitisation journey. The full, very detailed and VIGIE-aligned paradata and metadata record of every object and how each 3D model came to exist (or how it didn't!).
  • Lab journal is where the personal side lives — a day-by-day journal of the challenges, the dead ends, and what I actually from them along the way. I then captured my biggest takeaways from the project in Lessons learned. Hopefully it helps other peers avoid my same mistakes!
